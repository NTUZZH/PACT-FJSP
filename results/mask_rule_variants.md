# Three admissibility rules without training (scripts/p107_mask_rule_variants.py infer)

Rules: nondelay: admissible when the processing start is within half an hour of the earliest processing start over the compatible pairs (the rule of the paper); block: admissible when the block start (after the push past outages, before the setup) is within half an hour of the earliest block start over the compatible pairs (classical non-delay on block starts); active: admissible when the processing start is earlier than the earliest completion over the compatible pairs by more than half an hour (Giffler-Thompson). Difference: 100 (x_X - x_nondelay) / x_nondelay per case; negative means rule X gives the shorter schedule. Gap: 100 (replay_X - reference) / reference per case. Interval: stats_family.instance_boot, 20000 resamples, fresh default_rng(7); pooled, a drawn instance brings all its combinations. Pairs: along the SPT rollout under each rule: admissible pairs before every decision, over all decisions of all cases of the row. Rows: per combination; All: pooled over the selected combinations; Setup: pooled over S, LS, SW, LSW. Random control: 10 rollouts, default_rng(SeedSequence([20261113, code, instance index, draw])), codes pub10x5 105, 10x25 10, 50x25n30 50, 80x25n30 80.
