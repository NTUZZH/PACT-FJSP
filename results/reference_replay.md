# Replay of reference schedules under the non-delay mask (scripts/p105_reference_replay.py infer)

Exact mask (first_mask and mask_ties exact, non-delay). Orders: A (s_R(o), [m != m_R(o)], completion, m, j), smallest first; B ([m != m_R(o)], s_R(o), completion, m, j), smallest first; C (c_R(o), [m != m_R(o)], completion, m, j), smallest first. The replay of a case is the shortest of the three. Gap: 100 (x - reference) / reference per case; negative means x is shorter. Interval: stats_family.instance_boot, 20000 resamples, fresh default_rng(7); pooled, a drawn instance brings all its combinations. Shares: at or below: replay <= reference; within 1%: gap <= 1; above 5%: gap > 5. Buildable: % of references in the class (equal to the % that order A reproduces exactly). Outside the rule: mean over cases of the % of the operations of a reference that fail the class test: in total, because the operation starts later than its earliest start on its machine, and because another pair could start earlier. Off the reference: mean over cases of 100 x (decisions of order A off the reference) / decisions; the chosen pair (o, m) is off the reference when m != m_R(o) or an operation that R places on m_R(o) before o is not yet dispatched. Rule: per-instance best of FIFO, MOR, SPT and MWKR restricted to the legal pairs (stored exact-mask arrays).

CP-SAT configuration of cpsat_rule600: lns.
