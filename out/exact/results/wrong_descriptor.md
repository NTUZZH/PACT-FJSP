# Schedules under a wrong constraint descriptor (environment exact, training seeds 301,302,303,304,305)

Generated by scripts/p121_wrong_descriptor.py. 100 test instances of data/FAMILY/10x25 per seed, the policy xmask-v1-s<seed> under the exact non-delay mask (first_mask exact, mask_ties exact), one torch thread. Every schedule was re-checked by check C6 (the two validator modules).

## Cases

- A: environment and inputs from the true descriptor
- B: p40: environment from the true descriptor, descriptor vector from the configured one. The policy does not read the descriptor vector, so B gives the schedules of A decision for decision (checked in the rollouts); inputs computed from the configured descriptor while the environment simulates the true one would need an environment that no plant can configure. Not run.
- C: environment and inputs from the configured descriptor: what a plant with the wrong descriptor gets
- D: p40: environment from the configured descriptor, descriptor vector from the true one. For the same reason D gives the schedules of C. Not run.

Descriptor vector: at 23900 decisions the network was evaluated with the descriptor vector of the other combination of the pair; its output was identical at every one.
Reproduction: all 2000 makespans equal the stored arrays of test_results/FAMILY/10x25_xmask.

## Functions called

- rollout: eval_family.load_model, eval_family.mask_options, the greedy branch of eval_family.main (FJSPEnvFamily, greedy_select_action)
- validator: constraint_family.checks.check_schedule_validation (C6: schedule_validator.validate_schedule and reference_sim.validate_schedule)
- rule_buckets: scripts/p40_misconfig.py classify (loaded from its source)
- change: scripts/p88_family_inference.py rel: 100 (a - b) / b of the means
- pooled: arm_registry.pooled: per-instance mean over the seeds
